# Forget Camera

1. Click the `Forget` button on the desired camera device.

![Forget camera step 1 Image](./images/homepage_disconnections.png)

2. Click confirm.

![Forget camera step 2 Image](./images/confirm_forget_camera.png)

3. The camera should disappear from the UI.

![Forget camera step 3 Image](./images/camera_forgotten.png)

# Forget Edge Device

1. Click the `Forget` button on the desired edge device.

![Forget edge step 1 Image](./images/homepage_disconnections.png)

2. Click confirm.

![Forget edge step 2 Image](./images/confirm_forget_edge.png)

3. The edge device should disappear from the UI.

![Forget edge step 3 Image](./images/forgotten_edge.png)
